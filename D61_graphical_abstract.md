# Graphical Abstract

**File:** `figure/Fig1_conceptual.png` (the conceptual diagram used as Figure 1).

This conceptual diagram is offered as the Graphical Abstract for the JCE submission. It
shows the two-step (product-method) MR mediation indirect effect α̂β̂ and the two overlap
channels that make Cov(α̂, β̂) ≠ 0:

1. **Shared instruments** — the step-1 and step-2 instrument sets share SNPs (n_s > 0);
2. **Mediator–outcome sample overlap** — the mediator and outcome GWAS draw from
   overlapping samples (ρ_MY ≠ 0).

When either channel is present the traditional delta-method variance of the product is
biased, which the closed-form correction restores. Upload `figure/Fig1_conceptual.png`
as the standalone Graphical Abstract at submission.
